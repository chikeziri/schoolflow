# SchoolFlow OS

SchoolFlow OS is the financial operating system Inventa Labs runs for Nigerian private schools. Fees, books, payroll, and the parent’s payment all sit in one system, with each school kept separate.

Parents pay into a dedicated virtual account for the student or the family. When the money arrives, a signed notice from Paystack or Flutterwave is checked and posted into a double-entry ledger. Staff then run fees, accounting, banking, payroll, and reports. Inventa’s own administrators handle onboarding, pricing, settlement, billing, and the health of the platform.

**Live:** [schoolflowos.com](https://www.schoolflowos.com)

## Who it is for

School groups and proprietors who have outgrown a different spreadsheet for every campus, and parents who should see their own child’s fees without seeing the school’s internal books.

## What each person sees

- **Proprietor, bursar, and accountant.** Fees, the general ledger, banking, expenses, budgets, fixed assets, payroll preparation (including PAYE and pension), approvals, and reports.
- **Parent.** A login in the school’s own name. Their dedicated account. Credit that waits until they say which child it belongs to, when the family has more than one.
- **Inventa operator.** Which schools are live, what they are billed, how settlement is running, and whether payments and queues are healthy.

Platform charges are not shown to the school as if they were the parent’s fee.

## Why the money path is the product

A payment can arrive twice, or the notice can arrive late. SchoolFlow records a payment once. Amounts are kept in kobo, not in rounded naira. The school’s collections and Inventa’s own charges are not the same pot. If a parent’s bank name or email is shared across children, the credit still has to land on the right account.

Staff only see the jobs their role allows. Signing in is not the same as being allowed to move money.
